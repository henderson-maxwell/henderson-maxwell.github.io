Kusolva

Collaborative, auditable question banks — and the exam papers built from them.

Kusolva is a web app for exam-setting committees to auditably and securely collaborate on forming and maintaining question banks — along with their solutions, marking schemes, and rubrics — as well as the exam papers assembled from them. Built with multi-language content and UI support from the ground up.

What it does

Everything an exam-setting committee needs to build a paper together, securely.

  • Shared question banks

    Build and maintain a shared bank of questions, model solutions, marking schemes, and rubrics — organized and reusable across papers.

  • Auditable collaboration

    Every change is tracked and attributable, so committees can collaborate securely with a clear, reviewable history.

  • Exam paper assembly

    Assemble exam papers directly from the bank, ready for review, moderation, and sign-off.

  • Multi-language support

    Content and the interface itself support multiple languages, for committees and the candidates they serve.
